# 📊 Data Science Job Market Analysis (2025)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes **944 real-world data science job postings** to uncover the most effective strategies for job seekers in 2025. 

The goal was to determine the correlation between **company size, revenue, and offered salaries**, as well as to identify the specific technical skills (Tech Stack) that currently drive the highest market value.

## 🔍 Key Findings (Executive Summary)
After cleaning and analyzing the raw data, the following patterns emerged:

1.  **Salary Intelligence:**
    * **Average Salary:** €131,779
    * **Median Salary:** €134,724
    * *Insight:* The market is stable with a balanced distribution; there are no extreme outliers skewing the average.

2.  **The "Big Tech" Myth:**
    * **Correlation (Salary vs. Company Size):** `-0.08` (Negative/Neutral).
    * *Insight:* Larger companies do **not** necessarily pay more. Data shows that mid-sized tech firms often compete aggressively on salary against enterprise giants.

3.  **Top 3 In-Demand Skills:**
    * 🐍 **Python** (68% of jobs)
    * 🤖 **Machine Learning** (61% of jobs)
    * 🗄️ **SQL** (47% of jobs)

4.  **Infrastructure:**
    * **AWS** is the dominant cloud platform, significantly outpacing Azure and Google Cloud in job requirements.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used
* **Python:** Primary language for analysis.
* **Pandas:** Data manipulation, cleaning, and aggregation.
* **Seaborn & Matplotlib:** Data visualization (Heatmaps, Bar Charts, Distributions).
* **AST & Regex:** Advanced text parsing for cleaning "Skills" and "Location" columns.
